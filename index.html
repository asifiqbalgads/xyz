Engineering Excellence Since 1948

Thermalair Inc. bridges the gap between architectural vision and mechanical performance.

Fundamental to every building project are mechanical systems, which consume 40 to 60 percent of a building’s energy. Thermalair collaborates with integrated design teams to quantify desires for completed facilities in terms that match a client’s business goals and objectives.

Our engineers and skilled technicians evaluate design options, including first cost, operating costs, capital expenditures, and LEED® costs/benefits. The result is prolonged life for both individual parts and complete systems, resulting in less downtime and lower bottom line costs.
